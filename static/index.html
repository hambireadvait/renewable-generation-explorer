How it works

From a place on the map to a weekly total in three steps

  1. 1

    Pick a place or region

    Search by name, enter coordinates, click the map or draw a rectangle.

  2. 2

    Describe what's installed

    Solar capacity, tilt and losses; turbine rating, hub height and power curve.

  3. 3

    Get the forecast

    Weather is run through pvlib and a wind power-curve model, hour by hour.
